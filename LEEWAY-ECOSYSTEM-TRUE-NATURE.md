## What Each Layer Means

### Authority
**Purpose**: Who decides what is true, what is allowed, what is forbidden

**Manifestation**: Governance, Standards, Laws, Policies

**Question**: Who has the right to say "this is how it must be"?

---

### Identity
**Purpose**: Who am I, who are you, what are we

**Manifestation**: Agent identity, component identity, user identity, system identity

**Question**: Who is acting, and can I trust them?

---

### Discovery
**Purpose**: What exists, where is it, what is it connected to

**Manifestation**: Topology registry, knowledge graph, component map

**Question**: What is real in this ecosystem?

---

### Knowledge
**Purpose**: What do we know, what have we learned, what is true

### Learning

**Question**: How do we get better?

### Creation

### Execution

### Reasoning
**Purpose**: How do we think, decide, plan

**Question**: How do we exchange information?
